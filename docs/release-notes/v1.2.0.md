### Body

> ## What's new in 1.2.0
>
> ### 🌏 Bangla support
> The whole app and every bundled dhikr, routine and situational dua is now
> available in **Bangla** alongside English. Switch anytime from Settings, or
> pick your language on first launch.
>
> ### 📿 New guided collections
> - **Morning adhkar**, **Evening adhkar** and **Istighfar** as step-by-step routines.
>
> ### ✨ Also new
> - **Notes** — attach a personal note to any tasbih, from the counter or the library.
> - **Previous / next** buttons on the counter to move between tasbihs.
> - **Collapsible routine steps** — long routines stay tidy.
> - **Stronger borders** setting for higher-contrast, easier-to-scan screens.
> - Insights shows a **daily goal met** marker; default goal is now 500.
> - Tooltips and screen-reader labels on icon buttons.
> - Onboarding now lets you set language and theme up front.
> - Bundled Privacy Policy and a content disclaimer in Settings.
>
> ### 🐛 Fixed
> - Home and Insights no longer show stale content after a language switch.
>
> ### 🔧 Under the hood
> - More resilient encrypted-preferences handling.
> - Session tracking follows app foreground/background state.
>
> **Full changelog:** https://github.com/badhon495/dhikr/compare/v1.1...v1.2.0
